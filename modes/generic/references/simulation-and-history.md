## Authorities

Separate immutable identity, public facts, private truth, mutable state, belief state, event log, historical archive, and retrieval index. One fact class has one canonical owner.

## Actors and organizations

Actors require motives, constraints, resources, obligations, relationships, knowledge boundaries, availability, current goals, medium goals, long goals, and historical agency. Organizations require leadership, membership rules, resources, infrastructure, policy, internal interests, external relations, pressures, goals, and succession.

An important actor or organization must have an offscreen agenda. Player absence is not inactivity. Fame is not physical presence.

## Causal simulation

Prefer `pressure → decision → resource/state delta → affected actors → second-order consequence → recorded history` over unrelated number changes. Major outcomes must cite source events. Advantages require a cause, cost, exposure, decay, and counterplay. Failure, retirement, obscurity, schism, merger, and institutional death must be possible.

## Time and level of detail

Use domain-appropriate cadences. Maintain high detail for the current interaction, medium detail for the active locality or operation, periodic detailed ticks for important offscreen actors, strategic ticks for remote major organizations, and coarse updates for background populations.

Long skips produce a world delta derived from committed events. Cap growth, resource creation, and promotion so dominant states remain meaningfully scarce when the domain requires scarcity.

## History and succession

Death, retirement, removal, or disbanding does not erase IDs. Preserve successors, offices, debts, strategies, reputation, artifacts, records, and disputes. Historical summaries retain causal anchors and may expose biased interpretations without changing world truth.
