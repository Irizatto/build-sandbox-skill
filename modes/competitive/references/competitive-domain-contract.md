# Competitive domain contract

## Domain boundary

The domain contains competitors, players, coaches, teams, leagues, organizers, rosters, roles, maps, sides, rulesets, patches, drafts, matches, series, tournaments, seasons, standings, contracts, preparation, strategies, results, and evidence. Do not import unrelated fantasy institutions or progression language.

## Authority layers

Separate:

- official rules and administrative rulings;
- versioned game or competition data;
- recorded match observations;
- analyst estimates;
- team and player beliefs;
- private preparation;
- public audience knowledge;
- simulated outcomes.

Every time-sensitive fact needs source, timestamp, applicable competition, season or stage, ruleset, and confidence.

## Participants and organizations

Participants require stable identity, eligibility, roles, proficiency profile, preferences, form, health or availability where authorized, contracts, relationships, goals, knowledge scope, and history. Teams require roster rules, leadership, staff, strategy, resources, schedule, obligations, reputation, current pressures, and succession or roster-change processes.

## Time and history

Use match phase, game, series, stage, tournament, split or season, and multi-season cadences as appropriate. Preserve old outcomes under the ruleset that produced them. New patches or rule changes create new versions rather than rewriting history.

## Player roles

Support participant, coach, analyst, manager, organizer, scout, commentator, and spectator perspectives as requested. Each role receives different legal actions and knowledge projections.
